# Coordinate Policy

Owner: geospatial data steward. Approver: authority owner. Evidence: coordinate source, method, accuracy, and coverage report. Allowed methods are official-point, official-boundary-centroid, verified-boundary-centroid, named-place-mean, repository-reference, and missing. Blocking conditions: fabricated coordinates or completeness claims without coverage evidence. Rollback: supersede the release and restore the previous verified coordinate artifact.
